# LaunchDeckAI Product Brief

## Product Name

LaunchDeckAI

## Tagline

Your AI-Powered Launch Deck

## One-Sentence Description

LaunchDeckAI is a calm, structured mobile app that guides first-time app creators from launch confusion to launch readiness with AI coaching, guided blueprints, launch milestones, asset generation, and a promotional Signal Deck.

## Core Promise

Open the Deck. See the next move. Forge what is missing. Stage the signal. Launch with less chaos.

## Target Audience

LaunchDeckAI is designed for:

- First-time mobile app creators
- Indie React Native, Flutter, and no-code builders
- Solo founders launching an MVP
- Creators who feel overwhelmed by App Store and Google Play launch prep
- Builders who need structure, confidence, and AI help

## Emotional Job To Be Done

The user is not just buying a checklist. They are buying relief.

They want to feel:

- Less overwhelmed
- More prepared
- More confident
- Less afraid of missing something important
- Supported by an AI launch companion

## Product Positioning

LaunchDeckAI is not a generic project management app.

It is a guided launch clarity system for app creators.

## Core Screens

### Deck

The main command center. Shows what matters today.

### Missions

The structured launch roadmap and milestone checklist.

### AI Copilot Orb

The always-available Astro AI companion.

### Blueprints

Guided fill-in-the-blank launch worksheets.

### Foundry

AI asset generation hub.

### Cargo Bay

Saved launch asset vault.

### Signal Deck

A 16-step promotional launch sequencer.

### Launch Library

Curated resource hub for app launch links, guides, policies, and tools.

### Refuel Station

Plan upgrades, Fuel, and subscription management.

## What Makes LaunchDeckAI Different

1. It uses a launch-world metaphor that makes the app memorable.
2. It focuses on first-time app creators, not teams or enterprise users.
3. It combines launch readiness, AI creation, and promotional sequencing.
4. It makes marketing less vague through the Signal Deck.
5. Astro gives the AI experience a character and emotional anchor.

## v1 Success Criteria

LaunchDeckAI v1 is successful if a user can:

- Sign up
- Create a Mission
- Complete onboarding
- View a launch readiness score
- Follow launch milestones
- Fill in Blueprints
- Generate assets in Foundry
- Save assets to Cargo Bay
- Prepare Signal Deck content
- Ask Astro for launch guidance
- Upgrade from Cadet to Commander or Admiral
